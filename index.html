THE HEART OF C.F.H.

A compass, not a prescription.

What if finding your way wasn't about arriving at one perfect destination? Your experiences, relationships, values, and circumstances all shape how you navigate life—and your sense of direction can change.

Here, alignment isn't a test you pass or a permanent state you reach. It's an ongoing relationship with yourself and the world around you.

We can explore what feels true, what feels complicated, and what you might want to imagine next. You decide what the questions mean.

HELLO, I'M CAROLINE

The person behind the questions.

I'm curious about people: how we make meaning, find connection, and navigate the worlds we live in. My perspective has been shaped by sociology, education, cross-cultural experiences, travel, and a love of learning.

I bring that curiosity into a coaching partnership that is thoughtful, human, and sometimes delightfully playful. You don't need to arrive with a polished goal or a perfect answer.

I'm a certified Gallup CliftonStrengths coach. My Co-Active and ICF credentialing journeys are ongoing, not completed credentials.

And the word Collective? It reflects my belief that we are shaped by our relationships and communities—not a team of coaches or a membership program.
