MEET CAROLINE

A little curiosity can change the conversation.

I'm Caroline, the coach behind C.F.H. Alignment Collective. My work as a university sociology educator has shown me how powerful it can be to make room for questions, new perspectives, and those unexpected lightbulb moments.

My own path has been shaped by cultures, travel, learning, connection, and choosing meaningful work over someone else's definition of success. I bring that curiosity—and a healthy appreciation for humor—to our coaching conversations.

“Collective” reflects the importance of belonging, relationships, and community, not a team of coaches or a group coaching program. For now, coaching is one-on-one with me.

We won't search for a prescribed destination. We'll explore the compass that makes sense for you, knowing it may change along the way.
